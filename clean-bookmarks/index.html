The Problem

Your bookmark junk drawer,
turned into a clean tree.

Clean Bookmarks scans the loose items in Other Bookmarks and your Bookmarks Bar, asks an AI to propose a category structure, lets you tune it, then sorts everything into place. One click and it's all back the way it was.

Features

What it does

01

Two-pass AI

First proposes a taxonomy of 8–15 categories, then assigns every bookmark to it. Consistent across thousands.

02

You stay in control

Review the proposed categories. Rename, remove, or add before anything moves.

03

Non-destructive

Named folders are never touched. Results land in a dated 📁 Organized — YYYY-MM-DD folder.

04

One-click undo

The layout is snapshotted before any change. A single click restores it.

How it works

A small state machine

Toolbar popup

Organize

Kicks off the run.

→
Full-page tab

Runs the long job

Stays alive for multi-minute runs.

→
Service worker

chrome.bookmarks

Brokers fast read / apply / undo.

→
Your provider

The AI

The endpoint you configure.

WHY A TAB? MV3 service workers die after ~30s idle — that would interrupt a long run. The tab context stays alive instead.
Architecture
04
Pipeline

Read → Sort → Apply

01

Read scope

Collect the junk drawer. Named folders left alone.

02

Estimate

Batches, tokens, and dollar cost — shown before you spend.

03

Propose taxonomy

TypeBox-typed tool returns validated structured args. No JSON parsing.

04

Review

You edit the proposed categories.

05

Assign

Parallel batches by numeric index, validated against a Zod schema.

06

Apply

Snapshot taken, then bookmarks move into the dated folder.

07

Undo

Restore the snapshot at any time.

★

Resilient

Bounded concurrency, retries, safe Unsorted fallback.

Privacy

No backend.
No account.
No data store.

Your bookmarks stay in your browser. The only thing that ever leaves your device is bookmark titles and URLs — sent to the AI endpoint you configure.

Bring your own key
Stored in chrome.storage.local. Never synced.
Minimal payload
Only titles and URLs leave. Nothing else.
Scoped permissions
No install-time host access. Granted per-origin on first use.
